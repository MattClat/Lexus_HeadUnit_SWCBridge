LEXUS IS HEAD UNIT · SWC BRIDGE v0.9.2 · SOLVED OCT 2026

How SWC Bridge starts itself

The fix wasn't making the app start better. It was making the app not be running when you switch the car off.

01

The rule we discovered

When the car switches off, the head unit force-stops every running app, except the one playing music.

A force-stopped app is frozen. Android won't deliver it the boot signal or a single wheel press until someone opens it by hand.

Playing musicSpared ✓
Not runningNothing to stop ✓
Running in backgroundFrozen ✗
Swiped from recentsFrozen ✗ (any time)
02

Before vs. after

BEFORE · v0.7 – v0.8
Always running, so always frozen
1
A service kept SWC Bridge running all the time.
2
Car off: it was running, so the head unit force-stopped it.
3
Car on: frozen. No boot signal, wheel dead.
4
You opened it by hand, so it was running again… and the loop repeated.
v0.8 also hid it from recent apps. It didn't help: the cleanup goes by running, not by the recents list.
AFTER · v0.9.1 “LEAN MODE”
Runs only for a moment, so it survives
1
The app does its job, then shuts itself down a few seconds later.
2
Car off: it isn't running, so the cleanup skips it.
3
Car on: not frozen. Android is still allowed to start it.
4
Each wheel press wakes it on demand. You never open it.
The key insight: SWC Bridge doesn't need to be running to catch the wheel. Android starts it the instant a press is announced.
03

One wheel press, start to finish

You press NEXT on the steering wheel
Head unit announces it “a media button was pressed”
Android starts SWC Bridge in 30–50 thousandths of a second
Track skips in Poweramp plus volume topped up to 100%
App exits ~5 s later long enough for a double-press
04

A drive, from key-on to key-off

0 s · CAR ON
Full Android boot Switching off is a real shutdown, so every start is a fresh boot. The clock even resets to 1969.
~35 s
SWC Bridge runs, then exits The earliest Android lets any app run. It does its startup jobs and shuts down.
~55 s
Wheel presses arrive Earlier presses are held in a queue while Android finishes starting, then delivered at once.
CAR OFF
App isn't running The cleanup has nothing to freeze. Next start works on its own.
05 · THE TRAP ALONG THE WAY
Closing the screen the “clean” way froze it

v0.9 closed its settings screen by removing its own task. On this head unit, that counts as being swiped away: instant force-stop.

v0.9.1 just closes the screen and leaves the task alone. That's the version that passed every test.

06 · BONUS: VOLUME AT 100%
Top it up on every NEXT

Android ignored volume changes at startup because no music was playing yet. Your car amp sets the real loudness.

v0.9.2 checks the volume on each NEXT and raises it to 100% once music is playing. No pop-up, no adb.

07 · WHEN IT CAN STILL FREEZE
Anything that leaves it running at switch-off
✗Poweramp paused when you switch off
✗A wheel press in the last ~5 seconds before switch-off
✗The SWC Bridge screen open at switch-off
✗Switching off within ~1 minute of starting
Fix: open SWC Bridge once and it's back to normal. Habit: switch off with music playing.
